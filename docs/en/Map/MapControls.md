### 1. Map pan
Press and hold the left mouse button, then move the mouse to pan the map around the Earth’s center. Release the left button to stop moving.

![](/img/en-img/Map/MapControls/image-0.gif)

### 2. Map rotation
Press and hold the middle mouse button and move the mouse to rotate or tilt the camera. Ctrl + left mouse button also activates rotation control.

![](/img/en-img/Map/MapControls/image-1.gif)

### 3. Map zoom
Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom the scene. Press and hold the right mouse button and move the mouse up or down for quick scene zoom.

![](/img/en-img/Map/MapControls/image-2.gif)

### 4. First-person look around
Press and hold Alt + left mouse button and move the mouse. The camera position remains fixed while you look around in first-person view.

![](/img/en-img/Map/MapControls/image-3.gif)

### 5. Scene pan
Press and hold Shift + left mouse button and move the mouse to pan the entire scene horizontally and vertically without constraints from the Earth’s center. This is convenient for browsing tall models such as towers.

![](/img/en-img/Map/MapControls/image-4.gif)
