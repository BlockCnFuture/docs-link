![](/img/en-img/SharedSets/IncludeCenterLine/0.png)

In the strip mission parallel zigzag mode, you may configure whether to include the centerline during mission planning in advanced settings. It is disabled by default. When generating the flight path, flight segments are created on both sides of the centerline according to the overlap value.

If Include Centerline is enabled, the mission planner will first generate one flight segment along the centerline, then create additional flight segments outward to the left and right from the centerline following the specified overlap.
