![](/img/en-img/CommonSettings/WaylineDirection/0.png)

For Facade Wayline, the aircraft needs to fly in the vertical orientation. Limited by the gimbal roll range, custom wayline angles are not supported. Hence the **Wayline Direction** option is provided, allowing flight paths to be planned either vertical or horizontaL.
