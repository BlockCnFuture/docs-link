title: "Shooting Distance"
sidebar_position: 23

![](/img/en-img/CommonSettings/ShootingDistance/0.png)

Shooting Distance refers to the distance from the aircraft gimbal to the target surface.

For sloped-surface missions, it is the distance to the inclined plane; for geometry missions, it is the distance to the geometry surface; for close-range photogrammetry missions, it is the distance to the model surface.


Sloped-surface missions support the "Flip AOI" function. After clicking and saving, the flight path will be generated on the opposite side of the inclined plane.
