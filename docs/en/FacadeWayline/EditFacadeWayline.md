### 1. Draw Survey Area
In 3D mode, use the left mouse button on the map to define the base edge of the survey area. Move the mouse to adjust the inclination angle of the facade plane, then click the left mouse button on the map to confirm the survey area.

Right-click to undo one step during drawing.

Press Esc to restart drawing.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/0.gif)

### 2. Delete Survey Area
Left-click the survey area to activate it, then press Backspace or DELETE to delete the survey area.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/1.gif)

### 3. Edit Survey Area Vertices
Left-click the survey area to activate it.

### 4. Add Vertex
Click the ![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/2.png) button to add a new vertex at the button position.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/2.gif)

### 5. Delete Vertex
Right-click on a vertex ![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/3.png) to remove that vertex.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/3.gif)

### 6. Move Vertex
Hold the left mouse button on the vertex ![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/3.png) and drag the mouse to reposition the vertex. Release the mouse once the target position is reached to finish moving.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/4.gif)

### 7. Translate, Rotate and Scale Survey Area
Left-click the survey area to activate it.

#### a. Translate Survey Area
Hold the left mouse button on the direction axis ![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/4.png) and drag the mouse to translate the entire survey area. Release the mouse once the target position is reached to finish translation.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/5.gif)

#### b. Rotate Survey Area
Hold the left mouse button on the rotation ring ![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/5.png) and drag the mouse to rotate the entire survey area. Release the mouse once the target angle is reached to finish rotation.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/6.gif)

#### c. Scale Survey Area
Hold Ctrl + left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/3.png) handle and drag the mouse to scale the entire survey area. Release the mouse once the target size is reached to finish scaling.

![](/img/en-img/InclinedRoute/InclinedArea/7.gif)

Refer to the Common Settings section for other parameters.
