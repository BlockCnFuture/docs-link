### 1. Occluded wayline
When the wayline is occluded by terrain, buildings or models, the flight segments are displayed as dashed lines with orange warning color.

![](/img/en-img/WaylineManager/WaylineStyle/image-0.png)

### 2. Curved flight for waypoint wayline
When the waypoint wayline uses curved flight mode, the curved flight path may deviate significantly from the straight-line path. The straight-line path (non-executed path) is shown as dashed lines of the same color, while the curved path represents the actual executed path.

![](/img/en-img/WaylineManager/WaylineStyle/image-1.png)
