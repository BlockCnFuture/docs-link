![](/img/en-img/CorridorWayline/BoundaryOptim/0.png)

Boundary Optim can be enabled in the strip mission parallel zigzag mode.

After enabling Boundary Optim, the software will generate an additional flight line on both the left and right outer sides of the survey area to improve reconstruction results for parallel zigzag flight missions.
