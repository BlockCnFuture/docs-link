### 1. Draw Centerline
Use the left mouse button to define centerline vertices on the map. Then left-click the button ![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/0.png) or double-click the left mouse button to finish centerline editing.

Right-click to undo vertices during drawing.

Press Esc to restart drawing.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/0.gif)

### 2. Delete Survey Area
Left-click the centerline to activate the survey area, then press Backspace or DELETE to delete the survey area.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/1.gif)

### 3. Edit Centerline Vertices
Left-click the centerline to activate the survey area. Press ESC to exit the active state.

### 4. Add Vertex
Click the ![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/1.png) button to insert a new vertex at that position.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/2.gif)

You may also click directly on the target map location to append a new vertex to the end of the centerline.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/8.gif)

### 5. Delete Vertex
![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/2.png) Right-click on a vertex to remove it.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/3.gif)

### 6. Move Vertex
Hold the left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/2.png) vertex and drag the mouse to reposition it. Release the mouse once the desired position is reached to complete the move.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/4.gif)

### 7. Translate, Rotate and Scale Survey Area
Left-click the survey area to activate it.

#### a. Translate Survey Area
Hold the left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/3.png) button and drag the mouse to translate the entire survey area. Release the mouse after positioning to complete translation.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/5.gif)

#### b. Rotate Survey Area
Hold Ctrl + left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/3.png) button and drag the mouse to rotate the whole survey area. Release the mouse after rotation to confirm.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/6.gif)

#### c. Scale Survey Area
Hold Ctrl + left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/2.png) button and drag the mouse to scale the entire survey area. Release the mouse after adjustment to confirm scaling.

![](/img/en-img/StripRoute/StripArea/7.gif)
