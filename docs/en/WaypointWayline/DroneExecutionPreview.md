## Virtual Drone Lock / Unlock
When a waypoint is selected, the software automatically selects the last action of this waypoint. The virtual drone will snap to the waypoint position and enter the **Locked** state. In this state, the virtual drone’s position, attitude and other data align with the waypoint and its actions.

![](/img/en-img/WaypointRoute/DroneExecutionPreview/image-0.gif)


You can **unlock** the virtual drone by controlling it to fly away via keyboard, or right‑click on the map and select "Fly to here".

![](/img/en-img/WaypointRoute/DroneExecutionPreview/image-1.gif)


## Drone Attitude Preview at Action
After selecting a waypoint and locking the virtual drone, the virtual drone displays the drone’s attitude when the selected action is actually executed.

(Including nose heading, gimbal pitch, gimbal heading, gimbal roll, view frustum size)

![](/img/en-img/WaypointRoute/DroneExecutionPreview/image-2.gif)


## Drone Attitude Preview when Flying In / Out of Waypoint
After selecting a waypoint and locking the virtual drone, deselect any selected action by clicking it. The virtual drone will then show the drone’s attitude upon waypoint entry, before action execution, after action execution, and upon waypoint exit.

![](/img/en-img/WaypointRoute/DroneExecutionPreview/image-3.gif)

## Drone Attitude Preview after Modifying Waypoint / Action Parameters
With a waypoint selected and the virtual drone locked, any modifications to waypoint position, elevation and other parameters, addition or deletion of actions, or edits to action parameters will trigger an update of the virtual drone, showing its actual execution attitude.

![](/img/en-img/WaypointRoute/DroneExecutionPreview/image-4.gif)
