### 1. Draw Survey Area
Use the left mouse button on the map to define the vertices of the survey area. After selecting all vertices, double-click or click the icon ![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/image-0.png) on the vertex to finish drawing the survey area.

Right-click to undo one vertex during drawing.

Press Esc to restart drawing.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/0.gif)

### 2. Delete Survey Area
Left-click the survey area to activate it, then press Backspace or DELETE to delete the survey area.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/1.gif)

### 3. Edit Survey Area Vertices
Left-click the survey area to activate it.

### 4. Add Vertex
Click the ![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/image-2.png) button to add a new vertex at the button position.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/2.gif)

### 5. Delete Vertex
Right-click on a vertex ![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/image-3.png) to remove that vertex.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/3.gif)

### 6. Move Vertex
Hold the left mouse button on the vertex ![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/image-3.png) and drag the mouse to reposition the vertex. Release the mouse once the target position is reached to finish moving.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/4.gif)

### 7. Translate, Rotate and Scale Survey Area
Left-click the survey area to activate it.

#### a. Translate Survey Area
Hold the left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/image-5.png) handle and drag the mouse to translate the entire survey area. Release the mouse once the target position is reached to finish translation.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/5.gif)

#### b. Rotate Survey Area
Hold Ctrl + left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/image-5.png) handle and drag the mouse to rotate the entire survey area. Release the mouse once the target angle is reached to finish rotation.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/6.gif)

#### c. Scale Survey Area
Hold Ctrl + left mouse button on the ![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/image-3.png) handle and drag the mouse to scale the entire survey area. Release the mouse once the target size is reached to finish scaling.

![](/img/en-img/AreaRoute/MappingArea/7.gif)
